# Writing, review, scoring, and venue source reading

This note records the inspected source material and the concrete behavior incorporated in this skill. It is not an instruction to install or invoke another skill. All local scripts were written for this repository; no Sakana code was copied.

| Source snapshot and files read | Mechanism inspected | Implementation here and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer), local `SKILL.md` v4.0.0, MIT | Whole-document editorial pass: structural repetition, staged openings, formulaic contrast and closers, author-voice calibration, fact-preserving rewrite. | The [complete pattern catalog](../modules/humanizer/VENDOR.md) is now bundled with its MIT notice and applied by the [paper-writing workflow](humanizer-paper-writing.md) after scientific claims are settled. The skill rechecks numbers, methods, caveats, and citations and does not claim to evade AI detectors. |
| [K-Dense Scientific Writing](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/tree/main/skills/scientific-writing), local v2.3 `SKILL.md` through full core workflow, MIT | Evidence/claim manifest, local consistency checks, reporting guideline selection, human authorship and disclosure, confidentiality boundary. | The complete [offline toolkit](../modules/kdense-scientific-writing/VENDOR.md) is now bundled with MIT notice and is used within this skill's own paper workflow. The additional [manuscript ledger checker](../scripts/check_manuscript_ledger.py) validates declared claim-source links, method lineage and repeated numeric facts. Neither tool verifies actual passages or study conduct. |
| [K-Dense Peer Review](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/tree/main/skills/peer-review), local v2.4 `SKILL.md` core workflow, MIT | Intake and access, claims-to-evidence audit, design/statistics order, separate reporting versus validity, actionable review. | [Scoring rubric](scoring-and-venue-fit.md) separates soundness, evidence, transparency, clarity, novelty and fatal gates. The numerical score remains a reviewer judgment. |
| [Sakana AI Scientist V1](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist), local `ai_scientist/perform_writeup.py` and `perform_review.py`; custom license | Section drafting/refinement, related-work citation search, LaTeX/reference/figure checks; review form with numerical ratings and confidence. | The writing module checks section-specific claim and method consistency; [scorecard comparison](../scripts/compare_scorecards.py) adds evidence-locator and full-text gates plus weight sensitivity. We do not copy code or treat model review scores as validated quality measures. |
| [Awesome Conference Skills](https://github.com/CHB-learner/Awesome-Conference-Skills), local `skills/conference-router/SKILL.md` and venue-skill inventory | Candidate routing from contribution type and recent accepted-paper patterns; policy facts kept distinct from corpus patterns. | [Venue dossier](scoring-and-venue-fit.md#submission-opportunity-dossier-and-calendar) requires current official CFP, track, dates, fees and a backward plan. [Local checker](../scripts/check_venue_plan.py) checks only supplied time/cost structure. Historical accepted papers do not become official policy or acceptance odds. |

The present package does not include a live venue-policy database, a semantic source-to-claim verifier, or a validated scientific quality estimator. Those require current official sources, original papers, and accountable human review.
