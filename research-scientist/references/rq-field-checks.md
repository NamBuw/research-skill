# Field-specific checks before promoting a research question

Use this module after the general [RQ workflow](research-workflow.md) and [gap audit](gap-audit.md). It helps the agent ask for the right evidence in different fields without forcing every project into an experiment. These are starting checks; current field standards and domain expertise control consequential decisions. Adapted from the inspected [Good Question field playbooks](https://github.com/Rimagination/good-question/blob/main/docs/field-playbooks.md), with this skill's own evidence and engineering gates.

| Field | Specify before choosing an RQ | Strongest default attack | Informative early test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ecology and conservation | Organism/ecosystem, spatial and temporal scales, sampling frame, process, observation and management/theory stake | Site-specific pattern, confounding, scale mismatch, changing detection probability | Replicate a key pattern across sites or years; compare rival ecological mechanisms and sampling artifacts |
| Remote sensing | Sensor and acquisition dates, ground truth provenance/resolution, target variable, deployment region, simple baseline and decision use | Spatial/temporal leakage, label mismatch, map accuracy with no decision value | Cross-region or cross-year validation with audited ground truth and a simple model |
| Machine learning and AI for science | Scientific target, data-generating process, baseline, split, metric, plausible mechanism and belief or decision to update | Leaderboard gain mistaken for explanation; unfair baseline; engineering or metric defect | Matched baseline, leakage audit, stress test or ablation that discriminates mechanism from complexity |
| Social science | Population, construct, comparison, mechanism, identification strategy, measurement and ethics | Selection/confounding, construct invalidity, causal overstatement and unrepresentative sample | Measurement pilot and a comparison/negative control that tests a serious rival explanation |
| Biomedicine | Disease/process, model or cohort, endpoint, comparator, sample and translational decision | Underpowered design, indirect endpoint, confounding, post hoc mechanism and premature clinical claim | Feasibility/measurement pilot, appropriate control and a prespecified stopping signal; specialist and ethics review where required |
| Humanities and interpretive research | Corpus/archive, period, selection logic, debate, rival readings and interpretive method | Theory as decoration; selective archive; descriptive theme without a contested interpretation | Test the strongest counter-reading against a bounded primary-source set and an explicit archival boundary |
| Engineering and systems | System boundary, operator/user, baseline workflow, observed failure mode, constraints and deployment environment | Solution-first build; local bug called a gap; lab metric disconnected from operation | Reproduce the failure, audit the reference implementation, compare the simplest adequate alternative and define a deployment-relevant threshold |

For each row, distinguish what is documented from what is assumed. Search closest work with field-specific vocabulary and archives; check whether the apparent gap is a measurement, access, engineering, or scientific issue. Choose a pilot that can change the proposed question, not just demonstrate that a preferred method runs. If the pilot exposes a bug or invalid measure, return to [engineering diagnosis](engineering-vs-gap.md) before describing a field-wide limitation.

In cross-field work, write the structural analogy and its failure conditions. A method that works in one domain does not transfer its assumptions, outcomes or evidence standard automatically. Preserve the original field's prior art and the target field's constraints in the closest-work matrix.
